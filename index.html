My Story.

My journey into software started with curiosity — I wanted to understand how the things I used every day actually worked. That curiosity turned into a degree in Computer Science and never really stopped.

I started out building small tools as a freelancer, learning how to turn vague requirements into working software. Over time that experience pushed me toward more complex problems: distributed systems, API design, and building products that scale.

I'm most at home working across the full stack — designing a data model in the morning, wiring up a Liquid template in the afternoon. I believe great software comes from understanding both sides of the wire. Grimoire is a good example: a production-ready Shopify skeleton theme built for modularity and speed, following platform best practices from the ground up.

When I'm not shipping code, I'm probably reading about system design, experimenting with Go, or trying to understand why the last query was slow. I'm always open to interesting projects and collaborations.
